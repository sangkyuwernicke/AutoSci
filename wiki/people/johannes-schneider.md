## Research areas

Johannes Schneider is a researcher at the University of Liechtenstein (Vaduz) working on Generative AI, Agentic AI, foundation models, and explainability. His work includes surveys on GenAI foundations, computing paradigms, and the transition from generative to agentic systems.
