## Bio

Harang Ju is a researcher at the Johns Hopkins Carey Business School studying human-AI collaboration and organizational behavior. His work uses large-scale randomized experiments to understand how AI agents reshape work processes, productivity, and team dynamics.

## Recent work

- [[collaborating-ai-agents-field-experiment-teamwork]] — led development of the Pairit platform and the large-scale field experiment on human-AI teaming in ad creation.
