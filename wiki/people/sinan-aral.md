## Bio

Sinan Aral is a professor at the MIT Sloan School of Management and director of the Initiative on the Digital Economy. He is a leading researcher in digital economics, social network analysis, and the economics of AI. His work uses large-scale randomized experiments and causal inference methods to study how technology reshapes human behavior and organizational performance.
